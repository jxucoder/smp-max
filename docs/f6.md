# Order 6: f(6) = 48

The Lean theorem [f6_eq_48_of_unsat](../lean/SmpMax/Six/ExactMaximum.lean)
combines a kernel-checked witness of 48 with an upper bound conditional on
the unsatisfiability of every formula in `Cubes6.finalCubes`.
Normalization, encoding faithfulness and coverage of those cubes are proved
in Lean. The [architecture](architecture.md) explains the argument.

The campaign journal records cake_lpr acceptance for all 318,736 leaf cubes.
All 321,492 recorded formula hashes, including split parents, recompute from
Lean definitions. The journal is self-attested and its original certificates
were deleted; independent verification requires fresh solving and checking.
The [ledger](results.md) records the full evidence and the later 205-cube check.

## Verify the lower bound

From the repository root:

```bash
python3 tools/verify_witnesses.py results/f6/witness-48.json
```

Expected: `f6-48: 48 stable matchings; all three counts agree [OK]`.
The [structured witness](../results/f6/witness-48.json) is the dihedral
Latin instance of OEIS A351413. Its matrix gives each man's 1-based rank
for each woman; each woman's rank for that man is 7 minus the entry:

```text
123456
214365
365214
456123
541632
632541
```

Lean's [LowerBound.lean](../lean/SmpMax/Six/LowerBound.lean) defines
`dihedral6` and proves `dihedral6_count : stableCount6 dihedral6 = 48`
by kernel computation over the 720 matchings.
